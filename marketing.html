Top Movies of Summer 2024

  1. The Fall Guy - A romantic comedy starring Ryan Gosling and Emily Blunt, directed by David Leitch.
  2. Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes - The latest installment in the Planet of the Apes franchise, directed by Wes Ball.
  3. IF - A family film starring Ryan Reynolds, directed by John Krasinski.
  4. Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga - A continuation of the Mad Max franchise, directed by George Miller.
  5. Robot Dreams - An animated film known for its poignant storytelling without dialogue.
